# Appendix 5: MATLAB Programs for Money Management

The following two programs are written to calculate probability, expected value, total probability, total expected value and total expected value/average time of a trade, with a trailing stop-loss or fixed stop-loss. They are not necessarily written in the most efficient manner.

The parameters used in the Levy distribution in the programs correspond to the S & P 500 index. Those parameters need to be changed for calculation of other markets.
