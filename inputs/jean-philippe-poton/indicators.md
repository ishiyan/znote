# Jean-Philippe Poton — Definitive Chronological Indicator List

All indicators are published on Poton's blog [Fractal Finance](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/) and/or the [MQL5 Code Base](https://www.mql5.com/en/users/jppoton). The secondary blog is [Stochastic Fractals](https://stochasticfractals.wordpress.com/).

---

| # | Date | Indicator | Blog Post(s) | MQL5 | Description |
|---|------|-----------|--------------|------|-------------|
| 1 | 2009-02-15 | Fractal Adaptive Simple Moving Average (FRASMA, `frasma`) | [The speed of the FRAMA Pt 1](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2009/01/speed-of-frama-1.html) (2009-01-23), [Pt 2: The FRASMA](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2009/02/speed-of-frama-part-2-frasma.html) (2009-02-15) | [#8718](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/8718) | SMA with period adaptively scaled by trail dimension derived from FDI. Unlike Ehlers' FRAMA (which fractalises an EMA), FRASMA directly divides the SMA period by alpha = trail_dim/2 = 1/(2H). Trending markets speed up the MA; random/erratic markets slow it down. |
| 2 | 2009-04-21 | Fractal Graph Dimension Indicator (FGDI, `fgdi`) | [Fractal dimensions...And a FGDI](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2009/04/fractal-dimensionsand-fractal-graph.html) (2009-04-16) | [#8844](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/8844) | Corrected and improved version of iliko's FDI. Fixes two bugs in the original (loop boundary and denominator: ln(2(N-1)) instead of ln(2N)). Adds standard deviation bands around the dimension estimate as a confidence interval. Color-coded RED (trending, FDI<1.5) / BLUE (erratic, FDI>1.5). |
| 3 | 2009-04-26 | Fractal Adaptive Simple Moving Average v2 (FRASMAv2, `frasma2`) | [FRASMAv2](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2009/04/frasmav2.html) (2009-04-26) | [#8866](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/8866) | Updated FRASMA using the corrected FDI formula from FGDI. Adds a shift parameter to translate the output left or right. Core logic unchanged. |
| 4 | 2009-05-06 | Fractal Bands (FractalBands, `fban`) | [From Bollinger to Fractal Bands](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2009/05/from-bollinger-to-fractal-bands.html) (2009-05-06) | [#8895](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/8895) | FRASMA center line with upper/lower bands where the standard deviation multiplier is raised to the power H (Hurst exponent): bands = frasma +/- sigma * alpha^H. Wider than Bollinger during trends (H>0.5), narrower during mean-reversion (H<0.5). |
| 5 | 2009-05-07 | Fractional Bands (FractionalBands, `fctban`) | [Fractional Bands](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2009/05/fractional-bands.html) (2009-05-07) | [#8900](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/8900) | Strictly follows the FBM variance scaling law: deviation is raised to power 2H (instead of multiplying by alpha^H). Operates in PIP space (via PIP_Convertor) to avoid underflow with small real price values. For H=0.5, recovers standard Bollinger Bands. |
| 6 | 2009-10-21 | Rescaled Fractal Adaptive Simple Moving Average (RS-FRASMA, `rsfrasma`) | [Rescaled Range Analysis](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2009/10/rescaled-range-analysis.html) (2009-10-16) | [#9272](https://www.mql5.com/ru/code/9272) | FRASMA variant estimating the Hurst exponent via Rescaled Range (R/S) analysis instead of graph dimension. Partitions data into blocks at multiple scales, performs log-log regression of R/S vs block size. Period must be a power of 2. Computationally expensive. |
| 7 | 2010-04-05 | Fractal Dispersion (MTF_FractalDispersion, `fdisp`) | [Self-similarity and a measure of it](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2010/03/self-similarity-and-measure-of-it.html) (2010-04-15) | [#9604](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/9604) | Multi-timeframe indicator measuring dispersion (weighted std dev) of FGDI values across M5/M15/M30/M60/M240/M1440. Low dispersion = high self-similarity across timeframes = favorable entry conditions. Requires FGDI.mq4 as dependency. |
| 8 | 2010-05-17 | Hurst Difference (HurstDifference, `hurdif`) | [Variation of the Hurst Exponent](http://fractalfinance.blogspot.com/2010/05/variation-of-hurst-exponent.html) (2010-05-16) | [#9676](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/9676) | First difference (discrete derivative) of the FDI over time. Positive values indicate increasing volatility — potential entry signal. Based on the derivative of the FBM variance t^H(t). Does not indicate direction; must be combined with a directional indicator. |
| 9 | — | Fractal Bands Hybride Adaptive (FractalBandsHybrideAdaptive, `fbanha`) | — | — | Unpublished hybrid variant of Fractal Bands replacing fixed normal_speed with Ehlers' CyclePeriod indicator output multiplied by a Nyquist factor. Doubly adaptive: fractal dimension controls band width, dominant cycle controls center-line period. |

## Additional Notes

- Indicator #1 (FRASMA) was superseded by #3 (FRASMAv2) which uses the corrected FGDI formula.
- The FDI (`fractal_dimension.mq4`) by iliko is a prerequisite dependency, not a Poton indicator; Poton's corrected version is the FGDI (#2).
- Indicator #9 exists only as source code with no blog post or MQL5 publication.
- All indicators share a common theoretical foundation: the Fractal Dimension Index and the relationship D_G = 2 - H between graph dimension and Hurst exponent, and trail dimension D_T = 1/H.
