A few distinctions matter when preparing these masters:

- Autocorrelation: the existing autocorrelation-heatmap master (inputs/john-ehlers/prepared-for-conversion/autocorrelation-heatmap/description.md) implements February 2025 correlations of Ultimate-Smoothed prices. The book correlates Roofing Filter output. roofed-autocorrelation-heatmap makes that distinction explicit.
- DFT: zpano’s implementation (external/zpano/py/indicators/john_ehlers/discrete_fourier_transform_spectrum/discrete_fourier_transform_spectrum.py) subtracts a rolling mean and divides squared magnitude by period. The book uses roofed data and divides the sine/cosine sums by period before squaring, giving compensation proportional to period squared. The new master should make the book calculation authoritative.
- Comb spectrum: zpano supplies useful structure, but its default normalization and startup differ from the listing. Those differences need explicit documentation.
- Modified RSI: the Swami version clips its recursive output to [0,1] and retains the ratio on flat windows. Listing 7-5 leaves the output unclipped and skips its smoothing update when either denominator is zero.
- Hilbert: zpano uses seven-tap FIR estimators with period-dependent amplitude correction. Chapter 14’s revised construction uses a normalized one-bar difference. They deserve distinct references.
