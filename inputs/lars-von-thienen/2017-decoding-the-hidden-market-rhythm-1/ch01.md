## 1. IT’S ABOUT CYCLES

This book is about cycles. Cycles that influence our lives here on Earth. Cycles that represent energy flows affecting people’s moods and emotions. Cycles that represent energies originating from outer space. Cycles that manifest in the measurable value of the stock market. I want to introduce an approach to identifying relevant cycles and how to use this information for trading. This approach is not entirely new, but the way I use the “cyclic approach” is. Furthermore, I incorporate a few energy cycles from the universe into the mix. This combination is truly unique, as all details are explained, and this approach is decoded into a complete trading methodology.

Cycles are important.

Cycles surround us and influence our daily lives. Many events move cyclically. There is the ebb and flow of waves and the inhalation and exhalation of humans. Our daily work schedule is determined by the day and night cycles resulting from Earth’s rotation around its axis. The Moon’s orbit around Earth causes ocean tides. Cycles also impact women during their adolescent years—the menstrual cycle. Gardeners have long understood the advantages of working with cycles to ensure successful seed germination and high-quality harvests. They work in harmony with cycles to achieve the best results, the best crops. We experience four seasons each year, i.e., climate changes resulting from Earth’s rotation around the Sun. This seasonal cycle creates changing conditions that affect all living beings on Earth. A common cycle based on seasonal conditions is bird migration, a regular seasonal journey undertaken by many bird species.

You might wait until early spring to plant new seeds to take advantage of the pulsating increasing energy from warming spring temperatures. Knowing when the sun will rise may not seem like a prediction because we associate prediction with uncertainty and risk, but it is, nevertheless, a prediction of future events that is very precise.

These cycles are largely based on the cyclical movements of the Sun and Moon.

However, there is strong evidence that additional energy cycles in the universe influence our lives here on Earth. Independent research by the University of California and the University of Kansas revealed that the rise and fall of species on Earth appear to be driven by the movements of our solar system as it travels through the Milky Way. Some scientists believe this cosmic force may provide the answer to some of the biggest questions about Earth’s biological history (Schwarzschild, 2007). Finally, cycles have a long history in explaining our behavior on Earth. We can look far back in history to see that life follows a path of cyclical time. We can even find reference to this in the Bible:

> *A Time for Everything*
> *There is a time for everything, and a season for every activity under the heavens: a time to be born and a time to die, a time to plant and a time to uproot, a time to kill and a time to heal, a time to tear down and a time to build, a time to weep and a time to laugh, a time to mourn and a time to dance, a time to scatter stones and a time to gather them, a time to embrace and a time to refrain from embracing, a time to search and a time to give up, a time to keep and a time to throw away, a time to tear and a time to mend, a time to be silent and a time to speak, a time to love and a time to hate, a time for war and a time for peace.*
>
> Ecclesiastes 3:1–8

If there is indeed a time for everything, this also applies to people’s economic hopes, which manifest in the stock market’s value.

Two well-known pioneers who applied cyclic analysis to the stock market are WD Gann and JM Hurst. Gann used cyclical time and price patterns and geometric constructs but did not provide further details about his approach. His work remains a mystery to many of us.

Hurst was the first to introduce cycle analysis to technical analysis of the stock market. Even today, many cycle forecasters, such as Peter Eliades, successfully use techniques from Hurst’s seminal work *The Profit Magic of Stock Transaction Timing*. For example, Hurst demonstrated that the only difference between a head-and-shoulders pattern and a double-top pattern is the progressive elimination of the cyclic components. Additionally, a paper published by three authors from MIT’s Financial Engineering Laboratory in 2000 concludes that "technical patterns do provide information. It raises the possibility that [pattern] analysis can add value to the investment process" (Lo; Mamaysky; Wang; 2000).

Today, we have proof that pattern detection adds value to the investment process and that all technical patterns can be reconstructed using cyclic components. In this sense, it should be valuable to think in terms of cycles rather than using a framework consisting of static chart patterns. If this is the case—and it has been widely recognized—why do only a few traders and investors use cyclic analysis in the stock market? The likely answer is that cyclic analysis is extremely difficult to implement. It requires a great deal of work and some complex mathematics that are not easy for everyone to apply. Furthermore, there are many obstacles preventing traders from using cycle analysis:

1. **The Language Gap Between Cycle Researchers and Traders**
  One reason cycle analysis is often limited to scientific researchers is the language barrier. This is evident in the following example:
    1) *“The identified real support level, along with Fibonacci retracement, suggests strong short-term buying opportunities.”*
    2) *“The magnitude of the first six frequency patterns and the statistical significance of the Q-score suggest the presence of a predictable high-frequency component in the stock market.”*

    Although both statements convey the same meaning, most readers will understand the first statement but find the second puzzling.

2. **Knowledge Gap: Trading Expertise (Visual) vs. Cycle Calculation (Mathematical)**
  The second difference arises from distinct areas of knowledge. Technical analysis is primarily visual, while cycle analysis is primarily numerical. The visual mode of technical analysis is one of the few cognitive activities where humans still do not have an absolute advantage over computers. Numerical analysis involves studying datasets after the fact. However, in real-time environments, traders and investors must decide in the moment, and their decisions are largely based on visually recognizing patterns in charts. In many cases, the human eye can perform this “signal extraction” quickly and accurately. There are, or rather, only a few cycle tools available that can present the visual information extracted from numerical cycle analysis to the trader as a visual guide.

3. **Gap: Prediction vs. Trading (Different Market Approaches)**
  The third reason cycle analysis is rare in trading is the difference between forecasting and trading. Most traders are not interested in predicting the future; instead, they enter trades based on probabilities, apply money management, and exit trades by adhering to clear rules. They claim this is “the real path of trading.” Traders are convinced they can profit by entering trades randomly and applying money management and exit rules. On the other end of the spectrum are the “analysts.” This group is not interested in money management or exit strategies. They base their trading solely on predicting future market behavior. There is a gap in the mindset of these two groups, characterized by an ongoing debate about trading versus forecasting.

Cycle analysis is more of a forecasting method. Therefore, it is not surprising that this tool is not found in the toolbox of an active trader. The active trader is not interested in “prediction.” They manage their trades.

I aim to address these shortcomings with this publication—at least to some extent. Because ultimately, the essence of cyclic trading analysis is simple.

You do not need to understand how these results are achieved in your account. Everything is ready for use in the trading environment for traders who prefer everything in “visual” form. And the explanations I provide will give you the necessary knowledge and background on how to effectively use these tools.
