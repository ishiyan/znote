### 4.1 The Basic Idea Behind Using Dominant Market Vibration

Much has been written about tuning technical indicators using information about the dominant cycle’s duration. I will explain the fundamentals below.

The reason we typically use technical indicators with their standard length settings (e.g., 14 for RSI) is due to a lack of knowledge about how to determine the correct length in advance. One of the main drawbacks of using technical indicators with their standard settings is the problem of filtering out flawed trading signals.

Based on the logic of the calculation algorithm, standard indicators work best when their internal length parameter aligns with the dominant market vibration of the price movement being tracked. ‘Aligned’ means the length parameter is a harmonic number of the dominant wavelength. The easiest approach is to use the full or half harmonic of the measured dominant market vibration.

**Statement 1:** Set the length parameter of technical indicators to a full or half harmonic of the dominant market vibration.

Another common approach (when unsure of how to detect the dominant cycle) is to apply back-optimization to determine which length setting would have performed best in the past and use that value for future trading. However, even a back-optimized setting is a fixed value that cannot adapt to real market conditions.

Using fixed-length periods—whether back-optimized or standardized—in technical indicators leads to imprecise results on the right side of the chart, where all trading occurs.

Since dominant cycle lengths and phase shifts driving market change over time, indicator tuning must be done dynamically.

**Statement 2:** The length parameter must be adjusted dynamically.

However, the key to implementing this idea is having a reliable and precise method to detect the dominant cycle at any given moment. Most articles typically only explain how to adjust the standard length parameter of technical indicators and then leave you to figure out how to detect the real dominant cycle on your own.
