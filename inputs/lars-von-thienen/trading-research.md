# Trading Research: Lars von Thienen

## Biography

Lars von Thienen is a prominent cycle analyst, trader, and author based in Germany. He is associated with the **Foundation for the Study of Cycles (FSC)**, where he serves as a Board Member. He is known for his work in **cycle analysis**, **planetary cycles**, **W.D. Gann methods**, and **turning point forecasting** in financial markets. His expertise spans the intersection of cyclical analysis, technical analysis, and forecasting market turning points.

Von Thienen received his degree in **Industrial Engineering and Business Management** from the NORDAKADEMIE, University of Applied Sciences in Hamburg. He worked as a scientist, trader, and business consultant for over 25 years. Since 2005, he has been appointed as a commercial judge at the regional court of Hamburg. He has extensive experience in combining technical engineering know-how with economics and business-related issues, and runs his own private business consulting company.

He has been active in the trading community since at least 2012 (per ForexFactory references) and has contributed regularly to **Trader's World Magazine** since 2016. He is frequently cited alongside other cycle analysts such as **Andy Pancholi**, **Bill Sarubbi**, and the **Wave59** trading community. He has discussed market cycles with legendary technician **John Bollinger**.

Von Thienen is the author of a two-volume book series **WhenToTrade** on cycle analysis, and runs a popular Substack publication **Stock Market Cycles** with over 3,000 subscribers providing daily global cycle watch updates.

**Affiliations:**

## Books

### WhenToTrade Series (2 volumes)

Lars von Thienen is the author of a two-volume series on cycle analysis for financial markets, published by CreateSpace Independent Publishing Platform:

| # | Title | Author | Year | Publisher | Pages | ISBN | Links |
|---|-------|--------|------|-----------|-------|------|-------|
| 1 | Decoding The Hidden Market Rhythm - Part 1: Dynamic Cycles: A Dynamic Approach To Identify And Trade Cycles That Influence Financial Markets | Lars von Thienen | 2017 (3rd ed) | CreateSpace | 325 | 9781974658244 (pbk) / B074YDP92C (Kindle) | [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Decoding_the_Hidden_Market_Rhythm_Part_1.html?id=vLcQtAEACAAJ), [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B074YDP92C) |
| 2 | Decoding The Hidden Market Rhythm - Part 2: Metonic Cycles: A Non-Linear Approach To Identify And Trade Cycles That Influence Financial Markets | Lars von Thienen | 2014 (2nd ed) | CreateSpace | 238 | 9781499562590 (pbk) / B075LZJK8S (Kindle) | [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=1499562594), [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B075LZJK8S) |

**Part 1: Dynamic Cycles** builds upon research from J.M. Hurst, John Ehlers, and B.J. Millard, presenting a dynamic (adaptive) approach to dominant cycle detection using Digital Signal Processing. Includes trading examples on stock indices, commodities, forex, cryptocurrencies, and sentiment data. 3rd edition published August 2017.

**Part 2: Metonic Cycles** introduces correlations between markets and external energy cycles (gravity, geomagnetism). Uses DSP to reverse engineer Gann's master cycles, resulting in a 100% mechanical cycle-based trading system showing 20% CAGR over 30 years on the Dow. Includes TradeStation/EasyLanguage code.
