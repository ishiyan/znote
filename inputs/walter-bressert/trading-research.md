# Walter J. Bressert — Cycle Trading Pioneer

> Research compiled 2026-06-03. Every claim is tagged **[VERIFIED]** (supported by a cited source below) or **[UNCONFIRMED]** (plausible but not corroborated by a located source). URLs are real and were retrieved during research.

**One-line correction up front:** Bressert did **not** co-found the Foundation for the Study of Cycles. That organization was founded in 1941 by economist Edward R. ("Ned") Dewey. Bressert *studied* the Foundation's research and borrowed its "centered detrend" technique. He is, however, widely credited with **bringing cycle analysis into the futures markets**. (See Biography.) **[VERIFIED — his own TASC interview and bio pages]**

---

## Biography

Walter J. Bressert was a commodity/futures trader, analyst, educator, software developer, and newsletter publisher based in **Tucson, Arizona**. He is regarded as a pioneer of cycle-based market timing.

### Foundation for the Study of Cycles — clarification

In his March 1998 *Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities* interview, Bressert explains his relationship to the Foundation in his own words:

> "I found a couple of books published by Ned Dewey. Dewey had been hired by the US government back in the 1930s … After that, he left the government and started the Foundation for the Study of Cycles. … So I read their research on cycles. Plus, I studied The Catalogue of Cycles, which lists about 20,000 different cycles."

He also adopted the Foundation's **centered detrend** method:

> "I used a process called centered detrending … which was the process initially used by the Foundation for the Study of Cycles, and they still use it today."

So: **student/user of the Foundation's methods, not a co-founder.** **[VERIFIED]**

## Technical Indicators & Tools

### Core indicators / techniques (categorized)

| Indicator / Tool | Description | Category | Status |
|------------------|-------------|----------|--------|
| **Double Smoothed Stochastic (DSS / "DSS Bressert")** | A stochastic oscillator passed through two stages of EMA smoothing to remove the "wiggle" of a raw stochastic, giving cleaner cycle-turn signals. Co-attributed to **William Blau** and **Walter Bressert** (two slightly different formulations exist; "DSS Bressert" is the popular one). | Oscillator | **[VERIFIED — ProRealCode, LizardIndicators, TC2000]** |
| **Timing Bands** | Forecast windows. Bressert measured historical cycle low-to-low and low-to-high intervals, then kept the **middle 70%** of the distribution to produce a narrower, tradable "band" predicting when the next top/bottom should occur. | Cycle / Prediction | **[VERIFIED — TASC interview]** |
| **Centered Detrend** | Plot a centered moving average equal to the cycle length (e.g., 20-day MA plotted back 10 bars), then subtract it from price to expose cyclic tops/bottoms. Borrowed from the Foundation for the Study of Cycles. Lags by half the cycle length. | Cycle | **[VERIFIED — TASC interview]** |
| **Real-time Detrend** | A non-centered detrend usable at the hard right edge of the chart (less accurate than centered detrend, but no lag). | Cycle | **[VERIFIED]** |
| **3-10 Oscillator** | A detrend-style momentum oscillator (3- vs 10-period) overlaid on the detrend; tends to turn at cycle tops/bottoms. | Oscillator | **[VERIFIED]** |
| **RSI3M3** | His signature custom oscillator: a **3-period RSI smoothed by a 3-bar moving average**. Designed to flow with the detrend, dip below a buy line (~30) at cycle bottoms, and avoid the false signals of a raw stochastic. | Oscillator | **[VERIFIED — TASC interview, Fig. 2]** |
| **Setup bar / entry-stop pattern** | Two-step mechanical entry: oscillator drops below buy line then turns up → that bar is the "setup bar" (colored blue); a buy-stop one tick above its high triggers entry (red dot). Mirror image for sells. | Strategy | **[VERIFIED]** |
| **Left / Right Translation** | Reading cycle-top skew: right translation (top leans right) = bull trend; left translation (top leans left) = bear trend. Used to set trend bias. | Cycle | **[VERIFIED]** |
| **Controlled-Risk Money Management (multi-contract)** | Trade in multiples (2–3 contracts): scale out contract #1 quickly at a target to cut dollar and emotional risk; #2 targets the trading-cycle top; #3 rides the longer cycle. | Strategy | **[VERIFIED — TASC Fig. 1]** |
| **Profit-Taking Index (PTI)** | A Bressert exit/target indicator. Named in reseller/course materials and the user's brief; mechanics not described in the verified interview. | Oscillator / Exit | **[PARTIALLY CONFIRMED — name appears in third-party materials; not in primary sources located]** |
| **Overbought/Oversold Index & Momentum Index** | The two indices featured in *The Blue Book* (1981) subtitle. | Oscillator | **[VERIFIED — book subtitle]** |
| **Cyclefinder / CycleFinder Trader Pro** | Branded cycle-counting tool/indicator in the CycleTrader/ProfitTrader suite. | Cycle | **[VERIFIED — bressert.com testimonials, InSiide Track listing]** |
