# Dr. Belal Ehsan Baaquie -- Research Biography

## Summary

Belal Ehsan Baaquie is a theoretical physicist who spent the bulk of his career at the National University of Singapore (NUS), Department of Physics. He is best known for pioneering the application of quantum field theory and path integral methods to financial mathematics ("quantum finance"). He has published extensively with Cambridge University Press and is one of the few physicists to have created a sustained research programme applying quantum mechanics formalism to economics and finance.

## Research Areas

### 1. Quantum Finance / Econophysics (primary identity since ~2000)
- Application of path integrals, Hamiltonians, and quantum field theory to model financial instruments
- Interest rate models using quantum field theory (string-like models of forward rates)
- Option pricing via quantum mechanics
- Corporate bond pricing with quantum methods
- Microeconomic action functionals

### 2. Lattice Gauge Theory & Quantum Field Theory (1977--1990s)
- Migdal recursion equations
- Kac-Moody algebra realizations
- Lattice gauge theory Hamiltonians
- Asymptotically free gauge fields

### 3. Path Integrals & Quantum Mechanics Foundations
- Higher-derivative Lagrangians (acceleration actions)
- State space formalism
- Ising model path integrals
- Instantons

### 4. Islamic Finance
- Sukuk (Islamic bonds) with index-linked coupons
- Published in *World Scientific Annual Review of Islamic Finance* (2023)
- Affiliated with INCEIF, Malaysia
