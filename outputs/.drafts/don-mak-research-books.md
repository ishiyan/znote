### 1. The Science of Financial Market Trading

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Title** | The Science of Financial Market Trading |
| **Author** | Don K. Mak |
| **Publisher** | World Scientific Publishing Company |
| **Date** | March 20, 2003 |
| **ISBN-10** | 9812382526 |
| **ISBN-13** | 978-9812382528 |
| **Pages** | 260 |
| **Format** | Hardcover |
| **Amazon URL** | https://www.amazon.com/Science-Financial-Market-Trading/dp/9812382526 |

**Description:** Views the financial market from a scientific perspective. Reviews scientific literature on financial markets and describes mathematical procedures demonstrating that some markets are non-random. Discusses how markets are modeled phenomenologically and from first principles. Covers indicators (objective) rather than price patterns (subjective). Notes similarities between oscillator indicators and derivatives in Calculus. Introduces several new indicators with minimal time lag, explains divergences between price and momentum, and presents a skipped convolution technique for earlier market movement detection. Treats the market as a nonlinear phenomenon with emphasis on forecasting market turns.

## Summary

Don K. Mak has published **3 books specifically on financial market trading**, all with a strong mathematical/scientific emphasis:

1. *The Science of Financial Market Trading* (2003) - World Scientific
2. *Mathematical Techniques in Financial Market Trading* (2006) - World Scientific
3. *Trading Tactics in the Financial Market* (2021/2022) - Springer

All three form a coherent body of work applying scientific and mathematical methods (signal processing, nonlinear dynamics, calculus-based indicators) to market analysis and trading. The publisher World Scientific is an academic press based in Singapore; Springer is a major academic publisher based in Germany.
