> Research-only dossier on **Doug Schaff**, the forex trader/programmer behind the
> **Schaff Trend Cycle (STC)** indicator and founder of **FX-Strategy, Inc.**

## 1. Biography

Doug Schaff is an American foreign-exchange trader and software developer, founder,
president and editor of **FX-Strategy, Inc.**, a forex research and trading-tools firm
based in the Hudson Valley (71 Starbarrack Road, Red Hook, NY 12571). He is best known
as the creator of the **Schaff Trend Cycle** indicator, which he developed in the late
1990s and whose code he released publicly in 2008.

According to his firm's own biography pages, Schaff held an MBA from the University of
Chicago and brought "more than 25 years" of forex trading experience to FX-Strategy. His
institutional career included roles as a senior bank trader at **Bankers Trust** (New
York and Paris) and as **Chief dealer at Merrill Lynch Bank**, where he established the
bank's currency-options trading desk and traded dollar-yen OTC options. Earlier
biographical material also lists a senior partnership in currency options at **Refco,
Inc.** (from 1985), and credits him as the first person to trade a 10-year forex
(currency) option. He became a private/independent trader around 1987 before founding
FX-Strategy.

His firm describes him as "a pioneer in building technical forex trading tools, including
automated trading systems," explicitly naming the **Schaff Trend Cycle** and automated
systems on its Pro Charts platform — the clearest primary-source link between the man and
the indicator. Schaff co-authored two trade books outside his firm — *How to Make Money
Investing Abroad* (with Nancy Dunnan, HarperCollins, 1995) and *The Fundraising Planner*
(with Terry Schaff, Jossey-Bass, 1999) — and, with **Terry Schaff**, two FX-Strategy
titles: *The Four Elements of Successful Currency Trading* and *Getting Ready to Trade
FX*. A frequent FX-Strategy collaborator was technical analyst **Ian Copsey**.

**Confirmed:** nationality (American), education (MBA, Univ. of Chicago), employers
(Bankers Trust, Merrill Lynch Bank, Refco), firm (FX-Strategy, Inc.), authorship of STC,
2008 public code release.
**Not found / unconfirmed:** birth year, exact dates of birth, current activity status.

## 6. Indicator vs. person (clarification)

- **Schaff Trend Cycle**: created by Schaff ~1999; public code release **2008**
  (corroborated by a reader letter in *Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities*, June
  2010: http://traders.com/Documentation/FEEDbk_docs/2010/06/Letters.html).
- Schaff's only TASC bylines are two 1999 articles co-authored with **Walter Bressert**
  ("The Euro's True Colors," May 1999; "The Euro's Weekly Cycles," June 1999).
- The Investopedia STC article was authored by **Brian Twomey**, *not* Schaff (confirmed
  via archived capture).
