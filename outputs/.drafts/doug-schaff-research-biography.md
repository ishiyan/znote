# Doug Schaff — Forex Strategist, Creator of the Schaff Trend Cycle (STC)

> Research compiled 2026-06-03. Every claim is tagged **[VERIFIED]** (supported by a cited, retrieved source) or **[UNCONFIRMED]** (plausible but not corroborated by a located primary source). All URLs were retrieved during research and HTTP statuses recorded. Where a search tool was blocked, it is noted.

**Bottom line up front:** Doug Schaff is a verifiable, real figure in technical analysis — the named creator of the **Schaff Trend Cycle (STC)** indicator, and co-author (with cycle-trading pioneer Walter Bressert) of **two June/May 1999 TASC articles** on Euro cycles. His STC indicator has been ported to essentially every retail trading platform, which is the strongest objective evidence of his legacy. However, the *personal/biographical* record is thin: his birth year, education, photo, and any interview/video of the man himself could **not** be located. The widely-repeated bio line "Doug Schaff, founder/president of FX Strategy" is corroborated by multiple indicator-reference sites but could **not** be tied to the live `fxstrategy.com` domain, which was a parked/for-sale domain in 2005–2008 and later became an unrelated generic Joomla FX-affiliate site. No death record was found; current status is **unknown/quietly inactive**.

## Firm / Entity — "FX Strategy"

- **The name.** Multiple indicator-reference sites attribute STC to "Doug Schaff … who owns **FX-Strategy**, a company specializing in … software solutions for chart analysis" (LiteFinance) and to "Doug Schaff, founder/president of FX Strategy" (a line repeated across forex blogs). **[VERIFIED that the attribution exists; UNCONFIRMED as an independently registered company]**
- **The domain `fxstrategy.com` is almost certainly NOT his.** Wayback evidence:
  - 2002-01-22: "Web Page Under Construction." **[VERIFIED]**
  - 2005-11-24: "FXSTRATEGY.com — Premium Domain Names offered by Afternic.com" (domain **for sale**). **[VERIFIED]**
  - 2008-01-28: a **parked page** ("FxStrategy.com | Forex Secret Trading | FX Signal …" sponsored listings). **[VERIFIED]**
  - 2010–2011 onward: a generic **Joomla** FX-tips site ("The Ultimate FX trading site") with eToro affiliate banners — **no mention of Doug Schaff** anywhere on it. **[VERIFIED]**
  - The domain is still live (HTTP 200 as of 2025-11-17 in Wayback), but as an unrelated content/affiliate property.
- **Conclusion:** The "FX Strategy" associated with Schaff in STC attributions cannot be matched to the live `fxstrategy.com` domain. Either his firm used a different domain that did not survive, or "FX Strategy" is a descriptive/branding phrase that propagated through secondary STC write-ups. **[UNCONFIRMED — firm status/period; treat the fxstrategy.com link with caution]**
- `schafftrendcycle.com`: **no Wayback snapshots exist** — never an active archived site. **[VERIFIED negative]**
- No STC LLC or trademark record was located. **[UNCONFIRMED]**

### Where was STC FIRST published?

- **Not in TASC under Schaff's name.** STC does **not** appear as an article title or Schaff byline anywhere in the TASC archive (the only Schaff TASC footprint is the two 1999 Euro/cycle articles, which concern Euro weekly cycles, not the STC indicator per se). **[VERIFIED negative]**
- The consistent published account is that STC was **created in the 1990s** and its **algorithm "was made public in 2008"** (EarnForex, MQL5 CodeBase, mirrored widely). The earliest mainstream write-up located is the **Investopedia** article "Schaff Trend: A Faster And More Accurate Indicator" by **Brian Twomey**, live by **January 2010** (Wayback snapshots from 2010-01-17 onward). **[VERIFIED]**
- **Verdict:** STC's *first publication* was **outside TASC** — it propagated via the forex software/press ecosystem circa **2008**, then mainstream TA media (Investopedia, ~2009–2010) and the MetaTrader CodeBase. No single canonical "first paper" by Schaff himself was located. **[VERIFIED that origin is non-TASC, ~2008; exact first venue UNCONFIRMED]**
