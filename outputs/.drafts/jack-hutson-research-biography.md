# Jack K. Hutson — Biographical Research

## 1. Identity & Background

## 2. Role at Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities (TASC)

- **Role:** Founder and Editor
- **Company name:** Technical Analysis, Inc.
- **Founded:** 1982
- **Inaugural issue:** October 1982
- His byline in the very first issue (Oct 1982) reads "JACK K. HUTSON, EDITOR"

### Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| Late 1960s | Brief foray into the stock market |
| 1970s | Purchased two additional houses (real estate) |
| 1980 | Returned to securities trading |
| ~1980–81 | Purchased a personal computer system ($7,500) for charting; collaborated with Boeing colleague Anthony Warren (math PhD) to fix technical analysis software |
| Early 1982 | Invited to a technicians conference in Toronto; wrote a paper that became the foundation of the inaugural issue |
| October 1982 | First issue of *Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities* published |
| 1982–1983 | Worked full-time at Boeing, part-time on magazine |
| ~1983 | Magazine reached 1,500 subscribers at $250/year annual subscription |
| 1984 | Subscription price dropped; subscribers grew to 10,000+; Hutson resigned from Boeing to run magazine full-time (60–70 hours/week) |
| 1988 | Magazine had 12 employees; headquartered in the former Fauntleroy Elementary School building (Seattle) |
| 1998 | Profiled in The Seattle Times (still active at magazine) |
| 2013 | Magazine had 60,000 subscribers; 90% of articles by freelancers |
| Present (2026) | Magazine still publishing (traders.com); cumulative 1,226,237 subscribers from 174 countries since 1982 |

## 3. Published Work & Contributions

### Book
- *Charting the Stock Market: The Wyckoff Method* — published by Technical Analysis, Inc. (listed in their store/products)

### Notable Article Series
- **The Wyckoff Method of Trading Stocks** — 15-part series (Feb 1986–Dec 1987) in TASC
- **TRIX (Triple Exponential Smoothing Oscillator)** — introduced the TRIX indicator (Jul 1983, May 1984)
- **Using Fourier** (Jan 1983) — Fourier analysis applied to markets
- **Elliott Wave** — "Comparing Wave Predictions" (Feb 1989)

### Technical Contributions
- Developed/popularized the **TRIX indicator** (Triple Exponential Smoothing Oscillator) for technical analysis
- Collaborated with **Anthony Warren, PhD** (Boeing colleague, mathematics doctorate) on technical analysis software
