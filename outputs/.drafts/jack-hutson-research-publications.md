## 3. Other Publications & Contributions

### As Editor/Publisher of TASC

- **Founded TASC magazine in 1982** — served as editor and publisher from inception. The magazine has been in continuous publication since 1982 and claims over 1.2 million subscribers from 174 countries.
- Published numerous bound annual volumes (hardcover/paperback compilations of each year's issues) under his editorial credit.

- [UNVERIFIED] Hutson is widely credited as the creator/popularizer of the **TRIX indicator** (Triple Exponential Smoothing Oscillator), based on his 1983–1984 articles in TASC.
- [UNVERIFIED] He may have written forewords or introductions to other Technical Analysis, Inc. publications beyond the confirmed book above.

## 4. Summary

Jack K. Hutson's primary publication legacy is:

1. **Founding TASC magazine (1982)** — the first and longest-running magazine dedicated to technical analysis of financial markets.
2. **The Wyckoff Method book and article series** — a 15-part series (1986–87) later compiled into *Charting the Stock Market: The Wyckoff Method* (1991, ISBN 0938773062).
3. **Pioneering work on TRIX** — his articles on the triple exponential smoothing oscillator (1983–84) introduced this indicator to the trading community.
4. **Early articles on Fourier analysis, moving averages, and computer-aided trading** (1982–1984).
