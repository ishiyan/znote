## 10. Summary

Lars von Thienen is a German serial entrepreneur, cycle analyst, engineer, and honorary commercial judge based in Hamburg. He holds a degree in Industrial Engineering and Business Management from NORDAKADEMIE/TU Hamburg-Harburg. His 30+ year career spans programming, science, trading, business consulting, and judicial service. He founded bps business process solutions GmbH (1999), WhenToTrade.com (2005), and Noggle AG (2015). Since 2005, he has served as an appointed commercial judge at the Landgericht Hamburg. He joined the FSC Board of Directors in 2020 and hosts the weekly Market Cycles Report. WhenToTrade (his cycle analysis platform) is in the process of merging into the FSC. He has published three books, contributes to TradingVision software, and was nominated among the 50 Most Influential Business Leaders in Tech 2021.
