## 6. Indicators Attributed to Perry Kaufman

### KAMA — Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average

- **Category:** Adaptive MA
- **First Publication:** 1995 (*Smarter Trading*)
- **Formula:**
  ```
  ER = |Price[i] - Price[i-n]| / Σ(|Price[i] - Price[i-1]|) for i over n periods
  SC = [ER × (fast_SC - slow_SC) + slow_SC]²
     where fast_SC = 2/(2+1) = 0.6667, slow_SC = 2/(30+1) = 0.0645
  KAMA[i] = KAMA[i-1] + SC × (Price[i] - KAMA[i-1])
  ```
- **Description:** Adaptive moving average that speeds up in trending markets and slows down in choppy markets.

### Efficiency Ratio (ER)

- **Category:** Filter / Trend
- **First Publication:** 1995 (*Smarter Trading*)
- **Formula:**
  ```
  ER = |Close[i] - Close[i-n]| / Σ(|Close[i] - Close[i-1]|) for n periods
  ```
  Range: 0 to 1. Higher = more trending/efficient.
- **Also known as:** Generalized Fractal Efficiency
- **Description:** Measures the efficiency of price movement — ratio of net direction to total path traveled.

### Kaufman Volatility

- **Category:** Filter
- **First Publication:** 1995 (*Smarter Trading*)
- **Formula:**
  ```
  Volatility = Σ(|Close[i] - Close[i-1]|) over n periods
  ```
- **Description:** Sum of absolute price changes; the denominator of the Efficiency Ratio. Used as a noise/volatility measure.

### Adaptive Momentum (AMA-based Momentum)

- **Category:** Oscillator
- **First Publication:** Referenced in *Trading Systems and Methods*
- **Formula:** Momentum smoothed via KAMA algorithm
- **Description:** Standard momentum indicator with KAMA smoothing applied to reduce false signals.

### Adaptive Trend Detection (AMA with StdDev filter)

- **Category:** Trend
- **First Publication:** Referenced in *Trading Systems and Methods*
- **Formula:**
  ```
  Signal: |KAMA[i] - KAMA[i-1]| > k × StdDev(KAMA changes, n)
  ```
- **Description:** Uses standard deviation of KAMA changes to filter insignificant movements and identify true trend changes.
