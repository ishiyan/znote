## Career Summary

Raymond S. T. Lee has been active in research since the late 1990s, with **167 indexed publications** on DBLP spanning 1998--2026. His career divides into three major phases:

### Phase 1: Neural Oscillators, Pattern Recognition & Multi-Agent Systems (1998--2012)

His doctoral and early post-doctoral work focused on **chaotic neural oscillatory networks** for pattern recognition. Key contributions:

- **Lee Oscillators** -- a novel chaotic auto-associative network architecture (published in IEEE Trans. Neural Networks, 2004)
- **TCAN (Transient-Chaotic Autoassociative Network)** -- IEEE TNN 2004
- **Lee-Associator** -- chaotic progressive memory recalling (Neural Networks, 2006)
- **iJADE platform** -- a series of intelligent multi-agent systems:
  - iJADE Web-Miner (IEEE TKDE, 2004)
  - iJADE WeatherMAN (IEEE Trans. SMC-C, 2004)
  - iJADE Stock Advisor (IEEE Trans. SMC-A, 2004)
  - iJADE Surveillant (Pattern Recognition, 2003)
  - iJADE Authenticator, Face Recognizer, Reporter, etc.
- **SCENOGRAM** -- scene analysis via composite neural oscillatory elastic graph matching (IJPRAI, 2002)
- **Elastic Graph Matching** for face recognition and handwritten character recognition
- **MAGICS** -- mobile agent-based internet commerce system (IEEE Trans. SMC-C, 2007)

### Phase 2: Knowledge Engineering & Ontology (2007--2015)

- **KnowledgeSeeker** -- ontology modelling for information search (Springer book, 2011)
- Edited volume: **Computational Intelligence for Agent-based Systems** (Springer SCI vol. 72, 2007)
- Conference organization: SETE (Symposium on Emerging Technologies for Education), co-editor of proceedings (LNCS 13089, 2021)

### Phase 3: Quantum Finance & Chaotic Trading Systems (2017--present)

Founded QFFC and pivoted research to **quantum-inspired financial forecasting**:

- **Chaotic Interval Type-2 Fuzzy Neuro-oscillatory Network (CIT2-FNON)** for 129 financial products (IJFS, 2019)
- **CT2TFDNN** -- Chaotic Type-2 Transient-Fuzzy Deep Neuro-Oscillatory Network (IEEE Trans. Fuzzy Systems, 2020)
- **Quantum Finance and Fuzzy Reinforcement Learning Multi-agent Trading System** (IJFS, 2024)
- **LORSTransformerDRL** -- deep RL with chaotic oscillators for stock trading (IEEE Big Data 2025)
- **Dynamic Portfolio Optimization via Augmented DDPG with Quantum Price Levels** (arXiv 2501.08528, 2025)
- Chaotic neural oscillators applied to GCN for traffic forecasting (Applied Soft Computing, 2026; Neural Networks, 2026)
