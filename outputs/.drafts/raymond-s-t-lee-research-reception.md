### Structural concerns (analyst observations):
1. **Terminological appropriation**: The use of "quantum field theory" and "quantum entanglement" for financial forecasting has no established basis in physics or financial economics. The legitimate field of "quantum finance" (Baaquie, Haven, Khrennikov) uses quantum probability formalism as mathematical tools — Lee's work appears to go further in claiming physical quantum phenomena apply to markets.

2. **No peer validation**: Despite 150+ claimed publications, the specific quantum finance forecasting methodology has not been independently replicated or validated by other research groups.

3. **Self-referential performance**: All performance evidence is self-published on QFFC's own site. No independent fund performance audits, no regulated fund track record.

4. **No regulatory registration**: Neither QFFC nor Leanda Investment Group appears in Hong Kong SFC's public register. The site operates under a Chinese ICP permit and is hosted at a university subdomain, which gives it an academic veneer but does not constitute regulatory oversight of investment advice.

5. **Commercial model unclear**: The site offers free registration for forecasts but also has member-only content. The relationship between academic research and commercial advisory is blurred.
