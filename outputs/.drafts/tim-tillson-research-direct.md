## 9. T3 Formula (Verified)

### Generalized Double EMA (GD):
```
GD(x, v) = EMA(x) × (1 + v) − EMA(EMA(x)) × v
```

### T3 = Triple application of GD:
```
T3 = GD(GD(GD(price, v), v), v)
```

### Expanded form (6 EMAs):
```
EMA1 = EMA(Price, period)
EMA2 = EMA(EMA1, period)
EMA3 = EMA(EMA2, period)
EMA4 = EMA(EMA3, period)
EMA5 = EMA(EMA4, period)
EMA6 = EMA(EMA5, period)

c1 = −v³
c2 = 3v² + 3v³
c3 = −6v² − 3v − 3v³
c4 = 1 + 3v + v³ + 3v²

T3 = c1×EMA6 + c2×EMA5 + c3×EMA4 + c4×EMA3
```

### Default parameters:
- **Period:** 5 (varies by implementation; some use 12 or 14)
- **Volume factor (v):** 0.7 (Tillson's recommended value)

### Relationship to other averages:
| v value | Result |
|---------|--------|
| v = 0 | Triple EMA (EMA of EMA of EMA) — no DEMA component |
| v = 1 | Triple DEMA (Mulloy's DEMA applied three times) |
| v = 0.7 | **T3** (Tillson's recommended — balance of smoothness and responsiveness) |

### Properties:
- Smoother than DEMA/TEMA with lower lag than simple triple EMA
- Acts as a low-pass filter with steeper rolloff than traditional MAs
- Can overshoot price (unlike simple averages) due to v > 0
- Uses 6 internal EMAs but only 2 user parameters (period, v)

### Fulks/Matulich modification (2003):
Bob Fulks and Alex Matulich modified the T3 calculation to reduce overshoot and improve response. This is commonly offered as an alternative in MetaTrader implementations.

## Key Conclusions

1. **Tim Tillson is a one-publication author** — his entire public legacy rests on a single 5-page article in TASC (January 1998, pages 33-37).

2. **The T3 has extraordinary adoption** despite only one publication — 156+ MQL5 implementations, inclusion in TA-Lib and pandas-ta, and widespread community implementations across all major trading platforms.

3. **Tillson is an extremely private figure** — no photos, videos, interviews, LinkedIn presence, or biographical information is publicly available. Similar to Patrick Mulloy (DEMA/TEMA creator).

4. **The T3's mathematical elegance** (generalizing DEMA via a single volume factor parameter) likely accounts for its enduring popularity and ongoing development (adaptive T3, zero-lag T3, T3 deviation, etc.).

5. **The Fulks/Matulich modification (2003)** is the only significant refinement to the original formula, suggesting Tillson's design was already near-optimal.
