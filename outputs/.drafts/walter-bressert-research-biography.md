### Notable correction / nuance vs. the evidence base
The evidence base correctly states Bressert did **not co-found** the Foundation for the Study of Cycles (founded 1941 by Edward R. Dewey). However, his own career page adds that **"Walter was a director and president of the non-profit Foundation for the Study of Cycles"** — i.e. he held a *later leadership role* in the organization. The **director** role is independently corroborated: Merriman's event bio calls Bressert a "legendary cycles' analyst and **director with The Foundation for the Study of Cycles**" ([astrologyfla.com event page](https://astrologyfla.com/event/ray-merriman-forecast-2024/)). The **president** title appears only in his own bio. **[VERIFIED that he was a director; "president" UNCONFIRMED beyond self-report]**

**Verdict: UNKNOWN. No death record was located; he is documentably retired and out of public activity. Death is UNCONFIRMED; he may be alive but inactive.**

- **His own domain bressert.com has lapsed.** As of a May 2024 crawl, `bressert.com` 302-redirects to **HugeDomains** (domain for sale), so the son's older Bressert-branded vendor site is gone. **[VERIFIED — [Wayback 2024-05-22 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/2024/https://bressert.com/)]**
- **The business continues under the son, not Walter.** **Jerome Bressert** is "President at Cycle Trader Pro," U.S.-based, and on MQL5 he markets the indicators with the line *"My father, Walter Bressert made these DDS famous for years"* — past-tense framing of the indicators, published 2019. This implies Walter is no longer operationally involved but is **not** an explicit death statement. **[VERIFIED son runs it — [mql5.com/en/users/jbressert](https://www.mql5.com/en/users/jbressert); death not stated]**
- **No LinkedIn or social profile for Walter** was located. **[UNCONFIRMED]**

**Net:** The documentary picture is of a man who retired from active trading by the mid-1980s, wound down his public-facing work in the 2010s, let his domain lapse (~2023–24), and whose son carries on the brand. Whether Walter is living or deceased cannot be confirmed from any located source. Given the late-1960s start of his career, he would likely be in his 80s or older today, but **no birth year was found** to anchor this. **Do not assert death.**

## 4. Legacy & influence

### 4.1 The "brought cycle analysis to the futures markets" claim
This is the central legacy claim, and it is **repeated consistently across independent sources**, not just self-asserted:
- His own bio: "credited with bringing cycles into the futures markets." **[VERIFIED — aboutWB2.html]**
- Merriman Market Analyst calls him a **"legendary cycles analyst and researcher"** and dates the founding of an entire other firm (MMA, 1983) to studying under him. **[VERIFIED — [mmacycles.com/about-us](https://www.mmacycles.com/about-us/)]**
- *Futures* magazine (quoted on his bio): "He gets the readership he deserves because he is right so often." **[UNCONFIRMED original — quoted second-hand on aboutWB2.html]**

Assessment: **credible as a "popularizer/quantifier" claim.** Bressert did not invent market cycles (Dewey, Hurst, Gann precede him), but he is reasonably credited with **operationalizing** cycle theory for futures traders — adding Timing Bands, oscillator/cycle combinations, and a mechanical setup-bar/entry methodology that practitioners could actually trade. **[VERIFIED as a widely-repeated attribution; "first/sole" framing UNCONFIRMED]**

### 4.2 Influence on other analysts (direct lineage)
- **Raymond A. Merriman** — now a well-known financial-astrology/cycles author (MMA, est. 1983; five-volume *Ultimate Book on Stock Market Timing*) — explicitly began "in 1980 as an apprentice to Walter Bressert." This is a concrete, documented teacher→prominent-student lineage. **[VERIFIED — mmacycles.com/about-us; astrologyfla.com]**
- He taught "thousands" of brokers, traders and money managers via workshops (self-reported scale). **[VERIFIED self-report — aboutWB2.html]**

### 4.3 Citations by major TA textbook authors (Murphy / Kaufman / Pring / Ehlers)

### 4.4 Platform adoption of the DSS (the strongest objective proxy)
The **Double Smoothed Stochastic ("DSS Bressert")** is ported across virtually every retail charting platform — the clearest measurable evidence of lasting influence:
- **MQL4/MQL5 CodeBase:** a 13-strong DSS family, several explicitly Bressert-attributed (e.g. [code/8310](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/8310), [code/789](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/789), [code/23277](https://www.mql5.com/en/code/23277)). **[VERIFIED]**
- **TradingView:** multiple public Pine scripts (e.g. [HPotter's DSS Bressert](https://www.tradingview.com/script/W9J3VZUu-DSS-Bressert-Double-Smoothed-Stochastic/)). **[VERIFIED]**
- **ProRealTime, NinjaTrader, TC2000, MetaTrader, MetaStock, Trade Navigator** all carry DSS/Bressert ports. **[VERIFIED — per evidence base]**
- Commercial continuation: **CycleTraderPro** suite on the MQL5 Market by Jerome Bressert ([product/37936](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/product/37936)). **[VERIFIED]**

Caveat: the DSS is **co-attributed** to **William Blau** and Bressert; "DSS Bressert" is the popular branding but the credit is shared. **[VERIFIED — evidence base]**

### 4.5 Retail-community persistence
Sustained, years-long forum activity (ForexFactory, futures.io, NinjaTrader, MQL5, Trade2Win) around his DSS and cycle methods evidences durable *practitioner* interest decades after publication. Volume of threads = soft but real proxy for lasting relevance. **[VERIFIED — see evidence base Forum Discussions]**
