# William Blau -- Biographical Research

**Subject:** William Blau, technical analysis author  
**Known works:** *Momentum, Direction, and Divergence* (Wiley, 1995); TASC articles 1991--1993  
**Known contribution:** Inventor of the True Strength Index (TSI); pioneer of double smoothing techniques  
**Research date:** 2026-05-31

## 9. Academic / Professional Background

**Hypothesis:** Based on his work (double smoothing = cascaded exponential moving averages, which is a well-known digital signal processing technique equivalent to a second-order IIR filter), Blau likely had an engineering or physics background, possibly electrical engineering or signal processing.

**Evidence supporting this:**
- The double smoothing technique is mathematically equivalent to signal processing concepts
- His writing style in the book is described as having "a minimum of complex mathematics" -- suggesting he deliberately simplified for the trading audience, implying deeper mathematical knowledge
- The term "ergodic" (one of his indicators) comes from statistical mechanics/dynamical systems theory

**Result:** No academic publications, university affiliations, or professional engineering registrations were found.

## Conclusion

William Blau is a genuine enigma in the technical analysis world. He made significant, lasting contributions (the TSI is implemented in every major charting platform worldwide), published for a brief 4-year window (1991-1995), and then vanished completely. No photo, interview, conference appearance, obituary, patent, academic paper, social media profile, or personal website has ever surfaced. Even his publisher (Wiley) and magazine (TASC) pages contain no biographical information.

The most promising lead remains accessing the actual printed TASC articles from 1991-1993 or a physical copy of the book, both of which likely contain brief author biographical notes.
