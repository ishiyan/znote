# Deep Research Plan: risk-adjusted-returns

1. What are **risk-adjusted returns** in finance? Provide definitions and theoretical foundations.
2. What are the **key metrics** (Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Treynor Ratio, Calmar Ratio, Omega Ratio)?
   - Mathematical formulation
   - Intuition and interpretation
   - Assumptions and limitations
3. How do these metrics **compare** to each other?
   - Strengths and weaknesses
   - Situational applicability (e.g., hedge funds vs. mutual funds)

5. How are these metrics **applied** in portfolio management and trading strategies?
