## Key Questions

1. **Method substance** — What is Bressert's actual cycle-trading methodology, mechanically? (centered/real-time detrend, timing bands, RSI3M3, the oscillator/cycle "combination" thesis, controlled-risk money management). How does it relate to its antecedents — J.M. Hurst's cycle work and Dewey/Foundation for the Study of Cycles?

2. **The DSS attribution question** — Who actually originated the "Double Smoothed Stochastic"? Bressert vs. William Blau vs. others. What does the documentary record say, and why is it co-attributed? Resolve or fairly characterize the dispute.

3. **Biography & verifiable life facts** — What is actually documentable about his life, career arc, firms (HALCO / HAL Commodity Cycles / Walter Bressert & Associates), and current status (alive/deceased; son Jerome continuing the business)? Separate verified from folklore.

4. **Influence & legacy** — How influential was Bressert in technical analysis history? Evidence: citations by other authors, platform adoption of DSS, persistence of his methods in retail trading communities, the "brought cycles to futures" claim.

5. **Critical assessment** — Does the methodology hold up? Any independent/academic validation or critique of cycle-based timing and the DSS? Marketing-vs-substance caveats.
