# Gene Quong — Research Brief

## Biography

Gene Quong is a technical analyst best known as the co-creator (with Avrum Soudack) of the **Money Flow Index (MFI)**, a volume-weighted variant of J. Welles Wilder's Relative Strength Index (RSI). The indicator was introduced in the March 1989 issue of *Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities* (TASC) magazine, Volume 7, Issue 3, pages 76–77.

Very little biographical information about Quong is publicly available. He is not known to have published any books. His sole documented publication is the seminal 1989 TASC article co-authored with Avrum Soudack. The MFI has since become one of the most widely adopted volume-based oscillators in technical analysis and is included as a built-in indicator in virtually all major charting platforms (MetaTrader 4/5, MetaStock, TradingView, Bloomberg, etc.).

The core insight of Quong and Soudack was that RSI, which uses only closing prices, fails to reflect the participation intensity signaled by volume. By weighting price moves with volume, the MFI captures "money flow" — the rate at which capital enters or exits a security — and provides earlier divergence signals at market tops and bottoms.
