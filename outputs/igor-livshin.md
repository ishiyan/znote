# Igor Livshin — Research Brief

## Biography

Igor Livshin is a contributing author to *Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities* (TASC) magazine. He is known for developing the Balance of Market Power (BMP) indicator, which he introduced in the August 2001 issue of TASC. The indicator measures the relative strength of bulls versus bears by assessing their ability to push price to extreme levels. Livshin deliberately designed BMP not to be a range-bound indicator, making it equally sensitive at extreme points as at other levels. Beyond this single TASC publication, no books or other major publications by Livshin have been identified.

**Balance of Market Power (BMP)** — Also referred to as "Balance of Power" (BOP). Measures the strength of bulls vs. bears by assigning scores based on their daily ability to push price to extreme levels. The indicator oscillates between -1 and +1. A 14-period moving average is recommended for daily charts. Key properties include: during bull markets, tops cluster near the upper limit; during bear markets, bottoms cluster near the lower limit. Supports divergence analysis and trend identification.

Formula: `BOP = (Close - Open) / (High - Low)`
