# John Bollinger — Research Brief

## Biography

**John A. Bollinger, CFA, CMT** (born May 27, 1950, Montpelier, Vermont) is an American financial analyst, author, and the creator of Bollinger Bands, one of the most widely used technical analysis tools in the world. He was the first person to hold both the CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) and CMT (Chartered Market Technician) designations simultaneously.

### Career Timeline

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1950 | Born May 27 in Montpelier, Vermont |
| 1977 | Purchased first microcomputer; began computerized technical analysis |
| 1980 | Became an independent trader |
| ~1983 | Developed Bollinger Bands; first publicly presented on Financial News Network (FNN) |
| 1984–1990 | Chief Market Analyst at Financial News Network (FNN) for seven years |
| 1987 | Began publishing the *Capital Growth Letter* newsletter |
| 1988–1997 | Served on the board of the Market Technicians Association (MTA) — 10 years |
| ~1990 | FNN acquired by NBC; Bollinger helped transition to CNBC, continued as commentator |
| 1996 | Launched equitytrader.com — one of the earliest charting/TA websites (52-rule fuzzy logic model) |
| 1998–2003 | Served on the board of IFTA (International Federation of Technical Analysts) — 5 years |
| 2001 | Published *Bollinger on Bollinger Bands* (McGraw-Hill) |
| 2008–2012 | Served on the board of the MTA Educational Foundation (MTAEF) |
| Present | CEO of Bollinger Capital Management; operates BollingerBands.com |

### Key Facts

- **Credentials:** CFA, CMT — first person to hold both designations
- **Company:** Bollinger Capital Management (founder & CEO)
- **Website:** [BollingerBands.com](https://www.bollingerbands.com/)
- **TV Career:** Chief Market Analyst at FNN (1984–1990), responsible for all on-air technical analysis content; continued as regular CNBC commentator after FNN's acquisition by NBC
- **Coined "Rational Analysis":** The union of technical, fundamental, quantitative, and behavioral analysis — first used in the late 1980s, formally presented at AIMR Conference in 2004
- **Founding President:** Market Analysts of Southern California
- **Newsletter:** *Capital Growth Letter* (since 1987)
- **Industry group analytics:** Developed "Group Power," a proprietary equal-weighted industry group ranking system
- **Professional affiliations:** CFA Institute, Technical Securities Analysts Association, Market Technicians Association (CMT Association)

## Technical Indicators & Tools

### Core Indicators

| Indicator | First Published | Category | Description |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| Bollinger Bands | ~1983, FNN/TASC | Channel | Volatility bands plotted at ±2 standard deviations from a 20-period SMA. Bands widen with increased volatility and contract during quiet periods. |
| %b (Percent B) | 2001 (Book) | Oscillator | Normalizes price position relative to the bands: 0 = lower band, 1 = upper band. Values >1 or <0 indicate price outside bands. Formula: (Price − Lower) / (Upper − Lower) |
| BandWidth | 2001 (Book) | Oscillator | Measures band spread as a percentage of the middle band: (Upper − Lower) / Middle × 100. Used to identify Squeeze conditions. |
| BandWidth Delta | 2001 (Book) | Oscillator | Rate of change of BandWidth — the first derivative. Identifies acceleration/deceleration of volatility expansion. |
| Bollinger Band Squeeze | 2001 (Book) | Strategy | A pattern identified when BandWidth reaches a 6-month (125-day) low, signaling imminent volatility expansion and potential breakout. |
| BBTrend | Post-2001 | Trend | Trend indicator using Bollinger Bands calculated at two different lengths (default 20 and 50). Positive values = uptrend, negative = downtrend. |
| Bollinger Envelopes | 2001 (Book) | Channel | Variant of Bollinger Bands using different moving average types (EMA, etc.) instead of SMA as the basis. |
| Normalized Volume | 2001 (Book) | Volume | Volume divided by its average over a lookback period, providing relative volume context. |
| %b on Volume Indicators | 2001 (Book) | Volume | Application of the %b formula to volume-based indicators (MFI, OBV, etc.) rather than price. |

Bollinger Bands is one of the most universally implemented indicators in financial software. Below are major library and platform implementations:

### Open-Source Libraries

| Library | Language | Function/Class | Notes |
|---------|----------|---------------|-------|
| TA-Lib | C (with Python/Java/etc. wrappers) | `TA_BBANDS()` | Industry-standard TA library; implements upper, middle, lower bands with configurable MA type and deviation |
| pandas-ta | Python | `df.ta.bbands()` | Returns upper, middle, lower, bandwidth, and %b columns |
| ta (Python) | Python | `BollingerBands()` class | Part of the `ta` technical analysis library |
