## Key Decisions

1. Granville never authored TASC articles directly. All TASC content about OBV was written by other analysts referencing his work.
2. The "1960" vs "1963" publication date discrepancy: The Sep 2000 TASC article (Ehrlich) states Granville "originally published A Strategy Of Daily Stock Market Timing For Maximum Profit in 1960," while the better-known *Granville's New Key to Stock Market Profits* (1963) is the standard citation for OBV's introduction. Both dates are documented.
3. Larry Williams (TASC Mar 2004 letters) notes that the concept of cumulative volume predates Granville — it was presented by Woods and Vignolia in San Francisco in 1946. Granville popularized the term "on-balance volume."
