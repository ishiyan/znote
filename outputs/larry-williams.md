# Larry Williams — Research Brief

## Biography

**Larry Richard Williams** (born October 6, 1942, Miles City, Montana) is an American stock and commodity trader, author, technical indicator inventor, and former political candidate. He is one of the most recognized figures in the history of technical analysis and futures trading.

### Career Timeline

- **1942** — Born in Miles City, Montana
- **1960** — Graduated from Billings High School, Billings, Montana
- **1964** — Graduated from the University of Oregon School of Journalism
- **1965** — Began following the stock market
- **1966** — Developed Williams %R oscillator
- **1967** — Became a registered investment adviser
- **1973** — Made $1 million trading commodities in the bull market; published early work
- **1978** — Republican nominee for U.S. Senate in Montana (Class 2); lost to Max Baucus (56%–44%)
- **1979** — Published *How I Made One Million Dollars Last Year Trading Commodities*
- **1982** — Second Republican Senate nomination in Montana (Class 1); lost to John Melcher (54%–42%)
- **1985** — Published the Ultimate Oscillator in *Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities* (August 1985)
- **1987** — Won the World Cup Championship of Futures Trading (Robbins Trading Company): turned $10,000 into $1,137,600 (11,376% return) in 12 months trading futures
- **1997** — His daughter Michelle Williams (now a famous actress) won the same World Cup Trading Championship
- **1998–1999** — Published *Long-Term Secrets to Short-Term Trading* (Wiley)
- **2005** — Published *Trade Stocks & Commodities with the Insiders*
- **2010** — Tax case resolved: three felony tax evasion charges dropped; pleaded guilty to three misdemeanor charges of failing to file income tax returns on time (1999–2001)
- **2011** — Published *Confessions of a Radical Tax Protestor*
- **2014** — Recorded video series at Traders Expo Las Vegas discussing 50+ years of trading
- **2018** — Featured on NPR's Planet Money podcast (Episode 685: "Larry vs. the IRS")
- **Present** — Active trader and educator at ireallytrade.com; continues seminars worldwide

### Family

- Father of actress **Michelle Williams** (confirmed via Wikipedia, The Daily Telegraph) [1]
- Son **Jason Williams**, psychiatrist, authored *The Mental Edge in Trading* (McGraw Hill, 2012)
- Five children total
- Spouse: Carla Williams

### Other Notable Facts

- Co-founded the Rock 'N' Roll Marathon series (raised $320M+ for charities)
- Sponsored Montana Initiative 86 (first state to index tax brackets for inflation)
- University of Oregon journalism scholarship in honor of professor Max Wales
- Member of Sigma Chi fraternity (Significant Sig award recipient)

## Technical Indicators & Tools

### Core Indicators

| Indicator | First Published | Category | Description |
|-----------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| Williams %R | ~1966; popularized in 1973 book | Oscillator | Momentum oscillator showing close relative to high-low range over N periods; inverse of Fast Stochastic %K; scale -100 to 0 |
| Ultimate Oscillator | TASC August 1985 | Oscillator | Multi-timeframe momentum combining 7, 14, 28 period buying pressure/true range ratios with 4:2:1 weighting |
| Williams Accumulation/Distribution (W_A/D) | 1970s | Volume | Cumulative indicator: if close > prev close, add (close - true low); if close < prev close, subtract (true high - close) |
| Williams Variable Accumulation Distribution (WVAD) | Late 1970s | Volume | Volume-weighted: ((close-open)/(high-low)) * volume |
| Williams Commercial Index (Willco) | 2005 book | Volume | COT-based index measuring commercial trader positioning as oscillator |
| Will-Spread | 1999 book | Oscillator | Measures influence of secondary market on trading market |
| Volatility Channel (VC) | 1999 book | Channel | Channel based on 2*Typical - High/Low with period min/max |
| Greatest Swing Value (GSV) | 1999 book | Oscillator | Average of open-to-high and open-to-low swings; breakout signal when exceeded |

### Trading Patterns

| Pattern | Category | Description |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Oops! Pattern | Strategy | Gap-open reversal: market gaps past prior day's range then reverses back inside |
| Smash Day Pattern | Strategy | Close in lower portion of range after setup; reversal signal on next bar confirmation |
| Hidden Smash Day | Strategy | Variation with specific close-to-range and prior-bar relationships |
| Short-Term Swing Highs/Lows | Trend | Mechanical identification of turning points: high > adjacent bars' highs |
| %R Reversal Signal | Strategy | Specific divergence/reversal pattern in Williams %R readings |
| Outside Bar (Larry Williams variant) | Strategy | Bar engulfing prior bar used as breakout signal |
| Volatility Breakout | Strategy | Entry when current range expansion exceeds average recent range |
