# Manfred Durschner — Research Brief

## Biography

**Manfred G. Dürschner** (also transliterated as Durschner or Duerschner) is a German technical analyst and author. He is known for developing the **3rd Generation Moving Average**, a lag-reduction technique for moving averages. His work was published in the German-language financial press — specifically the article "Gleitende Durchschnitte 3.0" (Moving Averages 3.0), which appeared in a German trading magazine (likely TRADERS' Magazin, Germany). He holds a doctorate (referred to as "Dr. Manfred Dürschner" in some MQL5 implementations).

Dürschner does **not** appear in the English-language TASC (Technical Analysis of Stocks & Commodities) author archive. Despite the research brief being requested under the assumption he was a TASC author, exhaustive searches of the TASC author database, the TASC TOC XML archives (1990–2026), and web search engines confirm **zero English-language TASC publications** under any spelling variant (Durschner, Dürschner, Duerschner).

His influence in the English-speaking trading community comes entirely through third-party implementations of his 3rd Generation Moving Average on MetaTrader platforms.

## Technical Indicators & Tools

### Core Indicators

| Indicator | First Published | Category |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| 3rd Generation Moving Average (3GDMA) | "Gleitende Durchschnitte 3.0" (German, ~2007–2010) | Adaptive MA |

**3rd Generation Moving Average**: An advanced moving average that reduces temporal lag through a procedure based on increasing the MA period. Uses a parameter λ (lambda) where λ = 2 provides optimal lag reduction. Higher λ values increase similarity with the classic moving average. The technique can be applied to SMA, EMA, SMMA, and LWMA.
