# Raymond S. T. Lee — Deep Research Brief

## Executive Summary

Dr. Raymond S. T. Lee (Lee Shu Tong) is an Associate Professor at BNU-HKBU United International College (UIC) in Zhuhai, China, with a 25+ year career spanning chaotic neural networks, multi-agent systems, and — since 2017 — "quantum finance." His central contribution is the **Quantum Price Level (QPL)** framework, which repurposes quantum mechanics' Schrödinger equation as an analogy to identify discrete support/resistance levels in financial markets. He commercially deployed this system at Leanda Investment Group (2012–2017) and publicly via the Quantum Finance Forecast Centre (QFFC, qffc.org) since 2017.

Lee has legitimate academic credentials (PolyU PhD, 167 DBLP publications, h-index ~19, 5 Springer books) and publishes in peer-reviewed venues (IEEE Trans. Fuzzy Systems, IEEE Trans. Neural Networks). However, his quantum finance forecasting methodology has **no independent replication or validation**, no regulatory registration, and only self-published performance evidence. The "quantum" label is analogical — no quantum computing or true quantum physics is involved. One paper was retracted in 2026 [1].

## Quantum Finance Theory

### What Are Quantum Price Levels (QPLs)?

Lee's framework treats financial prices as analogous to quantum mechanical systems [11]:

| Quantum Mechanics | Financial Analogy |
|---|---|
| Electron energy levels | Discrete support/resistance price levels |
| Schrödinger equation | Eigenvalue equation on historical price data |
| Probability density \|ψ(x)\|² | Price probability distribution around QPLs |
| Quantum tunneling | Price breakouts through QPL barriers |
| Quantum entanglement | Cross-market correlation/co-movement |

**Computation method** (Ch. 5 of the book):
1. Treat price time series as a quantum system
2. Define a "potential well" from historical price distributions
3. Solve a Schrödinger-like eigenvalue equation
4. Eigenvalues → discrete Quantum Price Levels (predicted support/resistance)
5. Use QPLs for entry/exit decisions, stop-losses, and targets [11]

### The Quantum Trader (Multi-Agent System)

A modular architecture (Ch. 13) with specialized agents [11]:
- **QPL Agent** — computes quantum price levels
- **Trend/Momentum Agents** — chaotic neural oscillatory networks for direction
- **Risk Agent** — position sizing using QPL boundaries
- **Entanglement Agent** — monitors cross-market correlations
- **Execution Agent** — combines signals into trades

### How It Differs from Other "Quantum Finance"

| Dimension | Lee (QPL) | Baaquie (Path Integrals) |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | QM as analogy; Schrödinger eigenvalues | Rigorous QFT; Feynman path integrals |
| Application | Forecasting support/resistance | Derivative pricing, yield curves |
| AI integration | Heavy (chaotic NN, DRL, fuzzy) | Minimal (analytical math) |
| Validation | Self-published backtests | Analytical comparison to Black-Scholes |
| Reception | ~54 book citations; mostly self-citing group | ~500+ citations; mainstream math finance |
| Key books | Springer 2020 | Cambridge UP 2004, 2009, 2018 |

**Critical distinction:** Lee uses quantum mechanics as a *metaphor* — the equations are structurally similar but markets are not claimed to be literally quantum. Baaquie's approach is mathematically rigorous QFT applied to finance. Lee's strength is practical system engineering; Baaquie's is theoretical elegance [11][12].

## Reception & Criticism

### Positive Indicators
- Published in legitimate IEEE/Springer venues (IEEE TFS impact factor ~12)
- Book has 54 citations and 62k accesses on Springer [11]
- zbMATH review: "an excellent and fruitful book" (Grozdanov, 2020) [13]
- Commercially deployed for ~2,000 investors (2012–2017, per bio) [4]

### Concerns
1. **Self-referential ecosystem** — Most QPL citations come from Lee's own group; no independent replication [6]
2. **Analogical physics** — The mapping from QFT to finance is structural metaphor, not derived from first principles. Critics of analogical approaches argue the "quantum" label adds no explanatory power beyond classical support/resistance [12]
3. **No independent benchmarking** — No published comparison of QPL vs. Fibonacci, pivot points, or GARCH [5]
4. **Retraction** — One paper retracted in 2026 (Soft Computing) [1]
5. **Unregistered advisory** — Leanda Investment Group not found in SFC register; QFFC operates without financial regulatory oversight [5]
6. **Low external visibility** — No mainstream quant finance, physics, or financial press coverage found [5]

### Assessment

The absence of both strong endorsement and strong criticism suggests Lee's quantum finance occupies a niche with very low visibility outside its own ecosystem. The academic credentials are real, the publication venues are legitimate, but the specific forecasting methodology remains **unvalidated by independent parties**.
