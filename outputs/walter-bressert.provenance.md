## Corrections vs. folklore
- Bressert did **not co-found** the Foundation for the Study of Cycles (Dewey, 1941); he was a later director.
- The DSS that bears his name is, on the record, **Blau's**; co-attribution = two distinct formulations, not joint invention.
- *The Blue Book* (1981) true title/subtitle corrected from OpenLibrary metadata (initial BibTeX subtitle was fabricated and fixed before delivery).
