# Belal E. Baaquie — Trading Research Profile

## Biography

**Dr. Belal Ehsan Baaquie** is a theoretical physicist who spent most of his career at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**, Department of Physics. He pioneered the rigorous application of quantum field theory (Feynman path integrals) to financial mathematics, particularly interest rate modeling.

- **Education**: PhD likely Stanford University (SLAC affiliation, 1977; lattice gauge theory)
- **Career**:
  - ~1980s–~2016: Faculty, Department of Physics, NUS
  - ~2016–2018+: INCEIF (International Centre for Education in Islamic Finance), Malaysia
  - 2025: Still publishing (Quantum Economics and Finance journal)
- **INSPIRE-HEP**: 51 papers
- **Estimated h-index**: ~20–25
- **Google Scholar citations**: 634 (primary book alone)

### Key Mathematical Innovation

The forward interest rate f(t,x) is treated as a **two-dimensional quantum field** (calendar time × maturity). The action functional:

```
A[A] = -(1/2) ∫ dt dx · A(t,x) · [1/μ² + (1/λ²)∂²/∂x² + (1/κ⁴)∂⁴/∂x⁴]⁻¹ · A(t,x)
```

with 3 parameters (μ, λ, κ) provides an "almost exact fit" to Eurodollar futures correlation data, where standard HJM is statistically rejected.
