# Bill Wolfe — Wolfe Wave Creator

## Biography

Bill Wolfe is a former S&P 500 futures trader and the creator of the **Wolfe Wave** chart pattern, a naturally occurring harmonic pattern composed of five waves that implies an equilibrium price. He has been trading and teaching his methodology for over 30 years.

Wolfe's theory of wave structure is based on Newton's first law of physics (action-reaction). He uses Fibonacci spirals, natural cycles, and market geometry — no indicators, oscillators, or moving averages. His stated philosophy: *"I don't predict the market, I read the Waves."*

Early in his career, Wolfe had the good fortune of being introduced by **John Magee** (of Edwards & Magee's *Technical Analysis of Stock Trends* fame) to classic books and chart patterns. His son **Brian Wolfe** also trades the methodology and is credited as co-author of the Wolfe Wave manual. According to Linda Raschke (market wizard), Brian was "the first teenager I've ever met who consistently made a good income scalping NYFE futures from his apartment."

The Wolfe Wave pattern was first revealed to the public in the book *Street Smarts* by Linda Raschke and Larry Connor. Wolfe notes that many internet examples "bear no resemblance to what I currently teach" and that the pattern has been significantly improved since that publication.

Wolfe operates from Somers, New York (Westchester County), and charges **$3,000** for his course, which includes a PDF manual and 10 days of advanced email instruction. He offers unlimited support to students.

The **Wolfe Wave** is a registered trademark of Bill Wolfe.

## Technical Indicators & Tools

Bill Wolfe's sole contribution is the **Wolfe Wave** pattern. He does not sell software or indicators — per multiple forum sources, "Bill Wolfe does not sell software." He teaches a manual methodology based on drawing trend lines, Fibonacci spirals, and wave geometry.

### Core Pattern: Wolfe Wave

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **First Published** | Pre-2001 (via manual; first web reference 2002) |
| **Category** | Harmonic Pattern / Reversal Pattern |
| **Composition** | 5 waves (labeled 1–5) in a channel |
| **Basis** | Newton's first law, Fibonacci spirals, natural cycles |
| **Trademark** | Registered trademark of Bill Wolfe |
| **Used In** | Stocks, futures, forex, crypto |

### Pattern Rules

1. Waves must cycle at consistent time intervals
2. Waves 3 and 4 must stay within the channel created by waves 1 and 2
3. Waves 3 and 4 must show symmetry with waves 1 and 2
4. Wave 5 breaks out of the channel (false breakout)
5. A line from point 1 through point 4 predicts the target price
6. Entry at the completion of wave 5 (the "Sweet Zone" at Fibonacci extensions 141–261.8)
7. Stop loss below the 200 Fibonacci extension
8. 3-part position scaling: targets at points X, C, A

### Wolfe Wave vs. Elliott Wave

The Wolfe Wave is distinct from the Elliott Wave pattern. Key differences:
- Wolfe Wave numbers points 1–5 (Elliott uses 1–5 + A–B–C)
- Wolfe Wave requires wave 3 to be lower than wave 1 (bullish) — a >100% reversal
- Wolfe Wave provides specific target price via 1–4 line
- Wolfe Wave focuses on natural rhythm/cycles rather than fractal counting
