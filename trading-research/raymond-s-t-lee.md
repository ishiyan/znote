# Raymond S. T. Lee — Trading Research Profile

## Biography

**Dr. Raymond S. T. Lee** is an Associate Professor and IT Director at **Beijing Normal University–Hong Kong Baptist University United International College (UIC)** in Zhuhai, China. He is the founder of the **Quantum Finance Forecast Centre (QFFC)**.

- **ORCID**: [0000-0002-2487-1914](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2487-1914)
- **Education**:
  - B.Sc. (Physics), University of Hong Kong, 1989
  - M.Sc. (IT), Hong Kong Polytechnic University, 1997
  - Ph.D. (Computer Science), Hong Kong Polytechnic University, 2000
- **Career**:
  - 1989–1993: Meteorological Scientist, Hong Kong Government Observatory
  - 2000–2018: Lecturer → Assistant Professor → Associate Professor, Department of Computing, Hong Kong Polytechnic University
  - 2012–2017: Group CTO/Chief Analyst, Leanda Investment Group, China (applied Quantum Finance Forecast System for 2,000+ investors on major commodities)
  - March 2017: Founded QFFC ([http://qffc.org](http://qffc.org))
  - 2018–present: Associate Professor, BNU-HKBU United International College (UIC)
- **Statistics**: 150+ publications, 7+ textbooks/monographs, h-index 19
