# Tushar Chande — Trading Research Profile

## Biography

**Tushar S. Chande, Ph.D.** is an American quantitative trader, technical-analysis author, and money manager, best known for a family of adaptive and momentum indicators now standard on every major charting platform — the **Chande Momentum Oscillator (CMO)**, the **Variable Index Dynamic Average (VIDYA)**, the **Aroon** indicator, **StochRSI**, **Qstick**, and the **Chandelier Exit / Chande-Kroll Stop**.

- **Education**: Ph.D. in engineering (laser materials-processing / fiber-optics). **VERIFIED** indirectly: his 11 GE patents are all in laser/fiber-optics, consistent with this doctorate. MBA in finance — *widely reported but not independently confirmed in this pass.*
- **Engineering career**: Worked at **General Electric** R&D on laser materials processing — **VERIFIED**: all 11 patents are assigned to General Electric Company (see Patents section). The specific *Schenectady, NY* location (GE Global Research / Niskayuna–Schenectady) is consistent with GE's laser R&D but is not stated on the patents themselves — *treat the city as strongly inferred, not document-confirmed.*
- **Trading career**: Co-founded **Tuscarora Capital Management**, a registered Commodity Trading Advisor (CTA) — *reported; not independently confirmed in this pass (NFA/SEC and the book jacket bio were not reachable).* The Top Traders Unplugged guest bio states he "has founded several successful asset management firms." Long-time **Contributing Editor** of *Technical Analysis of STOCKS & COMMODITIES* (TASC), 1991–2021 — **VERIFIED** (see TASC list). Collaboration with veteran trader **Stanley Kroll** — **VERIFIED** via the co-authored 1994 book and the May 1993 TASC article.
- **Recent role**: **Co-Founder and Head of Research at Rho Asset Management** (Switzerland) — **VERIFIED**: so introduced in the 2014 Top Traders Unplugged podcast episodes (notes and audio).
- **Patents**: **VERIFIED — 11 U.S. patents** name Tushar S. Chande as inventor (9 as first inventor), all assigned to General Electric. The "nine patents" figure matches the count where he is first-named inventor (see Patents section).
- **Reputation**: Known for bringing an engineer's rigor to indicator design — emphasizing adaptivity (indicators that self-adjust to volatility), normalization, and statistically-grounded risk/stop methods.

### Signature Concepts

- **Adaptivity (VIDYA)**: A moving average whose effective smoothing constant scales with recent volatility (using CMO or standard deviation as the volatility proxy), so the average speeds up in volatile regimes and slows in quiet ones.
- **CMO normalization**: Unlike Wilder's RSI, the Chande Momentum Oscillator uses *un-smoothed* sums of up- and down-moves over the window, normalized to a ±100 range — `CMO = 100 × (Su − Sd) / (Su + Sd)`.
- **Aroon**: Measures *how long* since the highest high / lowest low within a lookback (time-based rather than price-based trend detection).
- **Volatility-based stops**: Both the Chandelier Exit and Chande-Kroll Stop place trailing stops a multiple of Average True Range (ATR) away from extreme highs/lows.

### Platform Availability

CMO, VIDYA, Aroon, StochRSI, Qstick, and the Chandelier Exit are **standard built-in indicators** on TradingView, MetaTrader 4/5, TradeStation, NinjaTrader, Thinkorswim, and most TA libraries (TA-Lib, pandas-ta).
